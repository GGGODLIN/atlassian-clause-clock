## Problem Statement

When a B2B SaaS provider experiences multiple service outages (Incidents) and resolves them in Jira Service Management (JSM), enterprise customers submit SLA Service Credit claims. Native JSM SLA timers only track single-ticket response and resolution targets; they cannot calculate a customer contract's monthly cumulative uptime across overlapping incidents minus scheduled maintenance windows, timezone-aligned billing months, prior approved credits, and monthly credit caps. Furthermore, Support, Finance, and Legal teams have no deterministic way inside Jira/JSM to apportion the resulting customer credit penalty across overlapping upstream vendor outage windows vs. internal operational absorption, leading to manual spreadsheet errors, over-issued credits, and unrecovered vendor liabilities.

## Solution

**ClauseClock** is a dual-target Atlassian Forge application (`jira:issuePanel`, `jira:globalPage`, `rovo:agent` with `rovo:skill` and `action`, `rovo:mcp` with open-source `SKILL.md`, `Teamwork Graph` read integration, and `@forge/realtime` live broadcast) paired with a shared-core standalone web studio for instant browser evaluation. Given a JSM SLA claim or monthly portfolio view, `ClauseClock` discovers linked incidents, scheduled maintenance windows, structured SLA contract tiers, and upstream vendor outage windows (via single-page Teamwork Graph reads, field-projected Jira REST queries, or 1-Click Seeded Scenarios), executes a pure-TypeScript integer-minute sweep-line and integer-cent largest-remainder engine to compute both the customer service credit (`Union(Incidents) \ Maintenance`) and upstream vendor penalty apportionment (`NetOutage ∩ UpstreamWindows`), emits a self-contained `CalculationReceipt` + `SourceProvenanceManifest`, and requires a version-bound human confirmation token before atomically writing the settlement receipt back to the JSM ticket, Jira Entity Properties, and Forge KVS ledger while broadcasting the update via `@forge/realtime` (with 1-Click Reset/Rollback support).

## Implementation Decisions

- **Single-Product Jira/JSM Installation Boundary & Structured Contract JSON (`1a`)**: The Forge app targets a single Jira/JSM installation (`jira:issuePanel` and `jira:globalPage` sharing one static client bundle) without requiring a mandatory multi-product Confluence link. Customer SLA contracts (monthly fee in integer cents, monthly credit cap in integer cents, billing timezone, prior approved credits in integer cents, SLA uptime tiers, and reference Confluence URL) are stored as validated JSON in Forge KVS / Jira Issue Entity Properties, eliminating brittle Confluence ADF table parsing and multi-app paid-listing lock-in `[user: "照你推薦"]`
- **Read-Only Single-Page Teamwork Graph + Pruned Jira REST + Seeded Fallback**: Because a single Forge app cannot simultaneously declare `graph:connector` and call `requestTeamworkGraph`, and because Teamwork Graph enforces `first <= 25` per page with a 25-second UI resolver timeout, the context loader uses a single-page read-only `requestTeamworkGraph` GraphQL query for graph relationship discovery, pairs it with `fields`-restricted Jira REST queries (excluding User objects to avoid the 7x point multiplier under the 65,000 points/hour Tier 1 global quota), and automatically falls back to 4 built-in deterministic seeded scenarios when a test site has no linked issues or Graph permissions, recording the exact source in `SourceProvenanceManifest` `[evidence: .scratch/clause-clock/brainstorm-review-2026-11-06.md Seat 1 Blocker 1 & Seat 2 Blocker 1-2]`
- **Pure-TypeScript Integer-Minute Sweep-Line & Largest-Remainder Cent Engine**: All time windows are normalized into half-open integer-minute intervals `[startMin, endMin)` clipped to the contract's timezone-aligned billing month window `[billingStartMin, billingEndMin)`. Three pure interval operators (`mergeIntervals`, `subtractIntervals`, `intersectIntervals`) compute both customer net billable downtime `NetOutage = (Union(Incidents) ∩ BillingWindow) \ Union(Maintenance)` and upstream vendor overlap slices `NetOutage ∩ Union(VendorWindows[i])`. All monetary values are computed in integer cents; when multiple upstream vendors overlap or share liability, the Largest Remainder Method apportions integer cents deterministically so that `sum(vendorApportionedCents) + internalAbsorbedCents === newCreditCents` holds identically. Any AI-supplied fee or credit override is ignored and logged in `discardedAiAmountOverrides` `[evidence: .scratch/clause-clock/brainstorm-review-2026-11-06.md Seat 1 High 4]`
- **Readable Version Key, Human Approval Gate, Atomic Single-Ticket Writeback & 1-Click Reset (`2a`)**: Every evaluated scenario produces a human-readable `proposalVersionKey` (`CCLOCK-${claimKey}-${billingMonth}-NET${netMinutes}M-CRED${newCreditCents}-VEND${vendorApportionedTotalCents}`, zero SHA-256/MD5 content hashing). Executing `commit-credit-settlement` requires a matching `APPROVAL::${proposalVersionKey}::${approverId}` token and current `expectedProposalVersionKey`; if missing or stale, it returns a structured `400` / `409` rejection (`MISSING_HUMAN_APPROVAL_TOKEN` or `STALE_PROPOSAL_VERSION_KEY`) with zero side effects. On valid approval, it checks the `(customerId, billingMonth)` cap ledger, atomically writes the `CalculationReceipt` (customer credit breakdown + vendor recovery apportionment) to the JSM ticket internal note, Jira Entity Property, and KVS ledger, publishes a compact notification on `@forge/realtime` (`publishGlobal`), and returns `idempotentReplay: true` on duplicate submissions. Executing `reset-settlement-state` restores the pre-settlement state so judges can re-test repeatedly `[user: "照你推薦"]`
- **Unified 4-Action Contract Registry & Single Forge Function Router**: To avoid Forge `manifest.yml` function-expansion limits (`ECO-1310`) and five-way schema drift across `action`, `rovo:mcp`, `rovo:skill`, `rovo:agent`, and TypeScript handlers, all capabilities are consolidated into 4 coarse-grained deterministic actions routed through a single dispatcher (`evaluate-clause-clock` `GET`, `run-pbt-audit` `GET`, `commit-credit-settlement` `CREATE`, `reset-settlement-state` `UPDATE`), backed by an open-source `skills/clause-clock/SKILL.md` (`name: clause-clock`, `allowed-tools` matching the 4 actions) and machine-readable receipt footers `[evidence: .scratch/clause-clock/brainstorm-review-2026-11-06.md Seat 1 Medium 6 & Seat 6 Blocker 2]`
- **Shared Client-Side Workbench (`ClauseClockHostPort`) & Edge-Case Ergonomics**: Forge Custom UI (`jira:issuePanel` + `jira:globalPage`) and the standalone Next.js web studio render the exact same pure client-side React workbench via an injected `ClauseClockHostPort` adapter. The workbench includes:
  - A 5-track aligned interval waterfall (Raw Incidents → Merged Union → Maintenance Deduction → Customer Net Outage → Vendor Overlap Slices);
  - Explicit edge-case banners for missing contract timezone (defaulting to UTC with an inline timezone toggle), unapproved/draft contracts (`Provisional Draft Mode` watermark that locks commit unless overridden), and monthly cap truncation (`Uncapped vs. Capped` badge);
  - Three 1-click Markdown export buttons (`Copy Customer Credit Receipt`, `Copy Vendor Recovery Claim`, `Copy Confluence Ledger Row`);
  - A split-view `@forge/realtime` live sync preview and embedded MCP & `SKILL.md` inspector `[evidence: .scratch/clause-clock/brainstorm-review-2026-11-06.md Seat 3 High 4 & Seat 6 High 3]`

## Testing Decisions

- **What makes a good test**: Tests exercise only the public action dispatcher `executeClauseClockAction(store, actionKey, payload)` and pure domain functions (`reconcileClauseClock`, `runClauseClockPbtAudit`, `validateClauseClockContract`), asserting observable outputs, receipt fields, invariant verdicts, and state transitions without mocking internal interval helpers.
- **Modules under test**:
  - Deterministic interval-algebra, SLA credit tiering, and vendor largest-remainder apportionment core;
  - 4-action dispatcher (`evaluate-clause-clock`, `run-pbt-audit`, `commit-credit-settlement`, `reset-settlement-state`), human-approval token gate, idempotency ledger, and 1-click reset;
  - Forge `manifest.yml` & `skills/clause-clock/SKILL.md` contract alignment and shared UI workbench rendering.
- **Prior art**: Node 24 native test runner (`node --experimental-strip-types --test`) with `fast-check`, following the 3-role PBT and human-approval token patterns established in `paypal-credit-roll-live`, `vultr-chart-truth`, `kiroween-signal-box`, and `qnx-vss-warden`.
- **Conditional PBT Specification (Shapes 2 & 4 — Conservation/Accounting & Order-Insensitive/Idempotent Interval Merge)**:
  - **Applicability domain**: Half-open integer-minute intervals `[startMin, endMin)` within a bounded billing month window `[0, 44640]` (`0 <= startMin < endMin <= 44640`), non-empty sorted SLA credit tiers (`0 <= creditBps <= 10000`), positive `monthlyFeeCents`, `0 <= priorApprovedCents <= monthlyCapCents <= monthlyFeeCents`, and 1..5 upstream vendor outage window sets.
  - **Independent Oracle boundary**: Reference minute-by-minute discrete bitmap oracle (`Uint8Array` of length `44640` where each minute index `t` is marked `1` iff `t` falls inside at least one incident interval and zero maintenance intervals, and vendor minute overlap is counted directly on the bitmap) that is algorithmically independent of the production sweep-line interval algebra.
  - **Mapped Invariants (`P1..P4`)**:
    - **`P1` (Interval Algebra Equivalence, Commutativity, Idempotence & Split-Invariance)**: The sweep-line `netOutageMinutes` equals the Independent Bitmap Oracle's minute count (`===`), is invariant under arbitrary permutation or duplication of input incident/maintenance intervals, and splitting any interval `[a, c)` into contiguous `[a, b)` and `[b, c)` produces the exact same `netOutageMinutes` and `newCreditCents`.
    - **`P2` (Monotonicity, Contract Fee Lock & Monthly Cap Bound)**: Adding an incident interval never decreases `netOutageMinutes` or `newCreditCents`; `0 <= newCreditCents <= Math.max(0, monthlyCapCents - priorApprovedCents)` always holds; and any AI-supplied fee override is discarded and recorded in `discardedAiAmountOverrides`.
    - **`P3` (Vendor Apportionment Cent Conservation)**: Across any set of upstream vendor outage intervals, `vendorApportionments.every(v => Number.isInteger(v.apportionedCents) && v.apportionedCents >= 0)`, `internalAbsorbedCents >= 0`, and `sum(vendorApportionments.map(v => v.apportionedCents)) + internalAbsorbedCents === newCreditCents` holds to the exact integer cent.
    - **`P4` (Idempotent Commit & Reset Round-Trip)**: Committing with a valid `APPROVAL::${proposalVersionKey}::${approverId}` token transitions the claim to `SETTLED`, deducts from the monthly cap ledger, and returns `idempotentReplay: true` on repeat calls without double-deducting; calling `reset-settlement-state` restores the exact pre-commit ledger balance and `READY` state.
  - **Three Test Roles**:
    - **Role 1 (Concrete Capability Examples)**: Verifies the 4 canonical seeded scenarios (15-second hero overlap folding `$5,000 -> $1,500` with `$900 / $600` vendor split, cross-timezone month boundary clipping, monthly cap truncation, and unapproved contract draft lock) plus missing/stale approval token rejection (`400`/`409`).
    - **Role 2 (Safety Property Guardians `fc.property` + Reachability Witnesses)**: Evaluates `P1..P4` against the Independent Bitmap Oracle across randomized interval sets and contract configs, returning `false` on any violation, and asserts aggregate `reachabilityWitnesses` (`overlapFoldedCount > 0`, `maintenanceDeductedCount > 0`, `capTruncatedCount > 0`, `multiVendorSplitCount > 0`) after `fc.assert` completes.
    - **Role 3 (Oracle Calibration & Counterexample Replay)**: Calibrates each `P1..P3` oracle check individually in test-local controls (verifying a test-local naive summation mutation trips `P1`, a test-local uncapped credit mutation trips `P2`, and a test-local unrounded float split trips `P3` without adding production fault switches), and pins a deterministic counterexample replay comparing the naive spreadsheet summation (`120m -> $5,000`) against `ClauseClock` (`75m -> $1,500`).

## Out of Scope

- Custom `graph:connector` ingestion or dynamic runtime Cypher string construction (prohibited by Teamwork Graph platform rules when calling `requestTeamworkGraph`).
- Live money transfers, ERP/SAP payment execution, or automatic legal liability admission (settlement commits record auditable internal notes, entity properties, and review receipts).
- Unstructured PDF/Word OCR or arbitrary Confluence HTML/ADF table scraping.
- Multi-currency FX conversion (all calculations operate in integer cents of the contract's base currency).
